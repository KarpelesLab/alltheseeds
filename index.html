alltheseeds

Every BIP39 seed phrase that can exist, listed in order, with the Bitcoin and Ethereum addresses it controls. Yours is in here somewhere.

How it works

A BIP39 phrase is just a number. A 24-word phrase encodes 256 bits of entropy plus an 8-bit checksum; each word is 11 bits. Row n is the phrase whose entropy is n: the first words spell out the number, and the last word is chosen so its checksum bits match SHA-256(entropy).

For each phrase the BIP39 seed is derived (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512, 2048 rounds, empty passphrase), then the first address on these paths:

  • BTC P2PKH uncompressed and compressed: m/44'/0'/0'/0/0
  • BTC P2WPKH (native segwit): m/84'/0'/0'/0/0
  • ETH: m/44'/60'/0'/0/0

Should I scroll for coins?

There are 2128 12-word phrases. Checking a billion per second, it would take about 1022 years to see them all, roughly a trillion times the age of the universe. The chance that any row you'll ever look at holds coins is, for all purposes, zero. That's what keeps seed phrases safe.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Even so, never type a real seed phrase into any website.
